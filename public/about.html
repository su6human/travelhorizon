We are from here.

TravelHorizon is a Bishkek-based team planning small-group journeys and locally led experiences across Kyrgyzstan.

Travel at the pace of the place

Kyrgyzstan is best understood slowly: a day that ends at a herder’s table, a pass crossed at the pace of a horse, a lake you have time to walk around.

We plan trips that leave room for that. Routes are short enough to enjoy, groups stay small, and the people who host and guide you are paid fairly and treated as partners.

How a trip comes together

  1. You send an enquiry

    Choose a tour or an interest and tell us how many people are coming and when.

  2. We reply with a proposal

    You get a route, dates, what is included and a confirmed price.

  3. You confirm, we prepare

    Once you agree, we arrange guides, transport and places to stay.

Booking and payment do not happen on this website. Every trip is confirmed directly with our team.
